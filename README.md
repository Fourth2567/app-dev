# My Favorite Show: Lanterns

## About
**Lanterns** (2026) is a DC Studios series on HBO, created for the screen by showrunner **Chris Mundy**. It follows two Green Lanterns as they investigate a murder in the American heartland, mainly in the fictional town of Rushville, Nebraska.

## Why I Like It
- A gritty *detective* mystery with cosmic stakes
- Described as a space cop version of True Detective
- A strong ensemble cast
- A grounded take on the Green Lantern mythos

## Main Characters
1. Hal Jordan (Kyle Chandler)
2. John Stewart (Aaron Pierre)
3. Supporting cast including Kelly Macdonald and Laura Linney

## Favorite Part
> The slow-burn murder mystery that reveals its cosmic side.
